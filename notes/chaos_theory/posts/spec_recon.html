Spectral Analysis of Dynamical Systems

Introduction

Spectral analysis of dynamical systems, as opposed to the geometric (takens embedding/state space) appraoch. Consider a discrete dynamical system \(f: X\to X\) with a measurement function \(h: X\to\mathbb{R}\) such that \[z_{n+1} = h(x_{n+1}) = h(f(x_n)).\] The koopman operator is an infinite-dimensional linear operator \(\mathcal{K}\) that satisfies \[\mathcal{K}h = h\circ f,\] acting on the space of observables via \[z_{n+1} = \mathcal{K}h(x_{n+1}) = h(f(x_n)),\] which becomes \[\mathcal{K}h(x_{n}) = \lambda \phi(x_{n})\] given an eigenfunction \(\phi\) of \(\mathcal{K}\) with eigenvalue \(\lambda\).
